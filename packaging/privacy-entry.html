Zeltwetter (Apple Watch) — Privacy

Zeltwetter is an open-source Apple Watch app that tells campers whether to put the rain fly on their tent tonight, from the night's forecast at their campsite. It runs on the watch alone. This section is the canonical statement of what Zeltwetter does and does not do with your data. The app is open source so that every claim here is independently verifiable. The repository is at github.com/excelano/zeltwetter.

The short version

Zeltwetter has no server of its own, no account to create, no advertising, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no third-party code. It talks only to Apple: to Apple's WeatherKit for forecasts and to Apple's map service for place names. Its App Store privacy declaration is “Data Not Collected,” because the developer receives nothing and has nowhere to put it.

What it reads

Location, with your permission and only while Zeltwetter is on screen, to fetch the forecast for where you are. Air pressure from the watch's barometer, with your Motion & Fitness permission, for a few seconds at a time during background refreshes between sunset and sunrise, and only while the pressure watch is turned on in Settings.

What leaves your watch

A camp's coordinates, sent to Apple's WeatherKit to fetch its forecast: your own position for the camp called Here, or the coordinates of a camp you saved. A camp's coordinates or your search text, sent to Apple's map service when you add a camp, to suggest a name and find its time zone, or to find places matching what you typed. A request for the Apple Weather mark, the logo the detail screen must show, from the address WeatherKit gives for it. These requests go from the watch to Apple through the system frameworks, under Apple's own privacy terms; the developer never sees them. Nothing else leaves the watch: there is no update check, no analytics service, no crash reporter and no remote logging.

What stays on your watch

Zeltwetter keeps the forecasts it has fetched, the camps you save with their names and coordinates, the nights you plan at them, your settings, and the barometer readings of the last twelve hours with the time of the last pressure warning. These live in storage the app shares with its own complication on the watch, are never sent anywhere, and are removed when the app is removed. Forecasts are dropped a day after their night ends; deleting a camp removes it and its planned nights.

Notifications

The pressure warning is a local notification raised by the watch itself. No push service is involved.

One thing outside the app's control

Apple collects its own figures about installs and, on devices where Share With App Developers is turned on, aggregate anonymous crash logs. Zeltwetter contains no code that touches that data, but Apple may still show the developer aggregate, anonymised numbers. On the paired iPhone the control is at Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, and it applies to every app, not only this one.

How to verify the claims yourself

The full source is at github.com/excelano/zeltwetter. The only network requests are made by Apple's frameworks: WeatherService in WeatherKitProvider.swift, CLGeocoder and MKLocalSearch in CampPlace.swift, and the AsyncImage that loads the Apple Weather mark in DetailView.swift. There is no URLSession call in the code. What it stores is written through Store.swift and SettingsStore.swift.
