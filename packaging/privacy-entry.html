Zeltwetter (Apple Watch) — Privacy

Zeltwetter tells you, on Apple Watch, whether the rain fly belongs on your tent tonight. To answer, it asks Apple for a forecast at your campsite. This section says what goes to Apple, what the app keeps, and how you can check both against the source code at github.com/excelano/zeltwetter.

Forecasts come from Apple

Each forecast is a request from the watch to Apple's WeatherKit carrying one camp's coordinates: your own position for the camp called Here, or the coordinates of a camp you saved. When you add a camp, its coordinates or the text you searched for go to Apple's map service, to suggest a name, find the camp's time zone, or list matching places. The detail screen loads the Apple Weather mark from the address WeatherKit supplies, because Apple's terms require it to be shown. All three go through Apple's system frameworks under Apple's privacy terms. Excelano runs no server for Zeltwetter, and nothing about these requests reaches the developer. Zeltwetter contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party code, and it has no account to create. Its App Store privacy declaration is “Data Not Collected.”

Your position

Zeltwetter reads your location only while it is on screen and only with your permission, to forecast for where you stand. Without that permission, camps can still be added by search or coordinates.

The barometer at night

With your Motion & Fitness permission and the pressure watch turned on in Settings, Zeltwetter reads the watch's barometer for a few seconds at a time during background refreshes between sunset and sunrise. A fast fall in pressure while the verdict is “Fly off” raises a notification that the watch itself posts; no push service is involved.

What the watch keeps

The fetched forecasts, the camps you save with their names and coordinates, the nights you plan at them, your settings, the last twelve hours of barometer readings and the time of the last pressure warning. They sit in storage the app shares with its own complication, never leave the watch, and go when the app is deleted. A forecast is dropped a day after its night ends, and deleting a camp removes its planned nights with it.

Apple's figures for developers

Apple counts installs on its own, and when Share With App Developers is on, it passes the developer aggregate, anonymous crash logs. Zeltwetter has no code that touches either. The switch is on the paired iPhone at Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements and covers every app.

Checking the code

Every network request is made by an Apple framework: WeatherService in WeatherKitProvider.swift, CLGeocoder and MKLocalSearch in CampPlace.swift, and the AsyncImage for the Apple Weather mark in DetailView.swift. The code makes no URLSession call of its own. Everything stored is written through Store.swift and SettingsStore.swift. This section is maintained as packaging/privacy-entry.html in the repository, and its git log is the record of every change.
